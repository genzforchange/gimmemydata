So much of what you do every day generates data…

data brokers

And once it’s collected, it doesn’t stay with the company you gave it to.

Data brokers buy it and combine it into a profile of you.

Your data is their product… and you’ve never even heard of them!

From that data, brokers generate thousands of inferences about you:

income: $74,000? renter: likely health: anxiety?
sold as fact

These inferences are guesses — your income, your housing, even your health — and they’re often wrong.

But they’re packaged and sold as fact.

The companies you shop with sell your information to data brokers — who resell your profile again and again.

$180/mo
insurers — your profile sets your rate
$60 $95
retailers — same product, your price
government agencies — surveilling your neighbors

ICE has bought data broker profiles to access personal and location information it would otherwise need a warrant to obtain.

What do they know about you? Where is it being used against you? Is it even correct?!! Should they even have this data about you?
