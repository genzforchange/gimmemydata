Request Your Data

A DSAR stands for Data Subject Access Request. It's your legal right to demand a copy of all the creepy, detailed info these companies have on you.

You can file DSARs with the companies people pay and shop with every day, from car brands, banks, and grocery stores to streaming services, utilities, and 401k funds, plus the data brokers who quietly trade our information behind the scenes. Request your data, take action, and help us build the case for stronger privacy for everyone.

FAQ / What to Expect

In most states, like California, companies have up to 45 days to respond. And they can request an extension for an additional 45 days to respond. Some requests can take a couple of minutes or days to get back to you.

Some companies will ask for a photo ID to "verify your identity" before sending the data. Other companies will ask for more detailed information about you, but that data legally can't be used for anything other than your data request.

Usually a big zip file full of CSVs or JSON files. You'll see every purchase, location ping, ad interaction, and customer service chat they've logged.
